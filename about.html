The Swung Out Story

Swung Out was born in 2021 out of a love for electronic music and the desire for founder, London-based Theon Bower to share more music with the world.

Over time, Swung Out has grown from a record label to also offering apparel, occasional events and even curating Spotify Playlists to share music beyond our own output.

We're always looking to collaborate, innovate and explore new ways of enjoying music. If you're a DJ, producer, curator, designer, you want to put on an event or even just want to chat, get in touch.

We hope you can join us for the journey – we're only in it for the music...
